No limit switches or homing. Global EN energizes all plugged-in drivers. Support vertical loads: disable, lost connection, or an electrical fault removes holding torque. Stop retains hold current.

Driver UART and temperature

Only drivers selected at the top of the sketch are queried. Each selected TMC must pass before Arm is allowed. “Seen” means UART responded, not that a motor is connected.

Temperature is the driver chip's threshold indication, not exact degrees or motor temperature. Thresholds: 120, 143, 150 and 157 °C. Prewarning / shutdown flags are shown separately. SG_RESULT is raw StallGuard data, not measured force.

Settings — all selected drivers

Disable outputs before applying. Current, precharge, start speed, step budget and StallGuard settings apply to all selected motors. Vertical and horizontal maximum speed and acceleration are independent. Values are RAM-only and reset on reboot.

Leave DIAG stopping off unless those wires are installed and validated. No limit inputs are used.

Current is per-motor RMS winding current, not supply current. The inactive stage stays at hold current. Only the moving stage receives stationary run-current precharge.
